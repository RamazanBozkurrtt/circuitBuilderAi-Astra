# Manufacturing

Follow [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) and the product document's DFM/DFA
and EOL sections. Control exact MPN/alternates, DNP options, assembly
orientation, board-pair traceability, fixture access and release artifacts.
Keep calculated/datasheet-backed validation separate from physical EVT,
DVT, PVT and factory EOL evidence. Do not treat a clean ERC/DRC or an
unmeasured protection concept as a production release.
