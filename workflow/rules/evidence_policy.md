# Evidence policy

Follow [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) and the current product/phase contract.
Manufacturer datasheets, manuals and errata outrank secondary sources for
electrical claims. Cite document revision and exact section/page when
practical. Record an unsupported claim as UNKNOWN and a source disagreement
as CONFLICTING. Graphify may locate files; it is not electrical evidence.
Keep physical measurements distinct from calculations and datasheet limits.
