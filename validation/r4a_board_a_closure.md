## 1. Four microphone channels: selected circuit and limiting corners

Use four electrically identical IM73A135 ports. Each differential leg enters a 100-ohm series resistor and one channel of an `OPA2320AIDGKR` dual op amp in unity feedback (four dual packages total). Put `TPD2E2U06DRLR` dual ESD protection at each connector, ahead of the buffer. Each buffer output passes a 47-ohm series resistor, with 1 nF to analog ground on the ADC side, then a separate 10-uF, 5% film AC-coupling capacitor into its TLV320ADC5140 differential input. `MKS2B051001N00JSSD` is a procurement candidate for each film capacitor; its current WIMA manufacturer availability and full temperature capacitance are not established, so it is not a released BOM item. ADC profile: AVDD/IOVDD 3.3 V; VREF 2.75 V; all four channel input impedances set to 20 kohm *typical* (P0_R60/65/70/75 bits 3:2 = `10`); PGA 0 dB; DRE/AGC disabled for four channels at 96 kHz. Set P0_R107 bits 1:0 to `00` with the default all-pass coefficients at P4_R72-83: the default HPF selection would otherwise cut at 24 Hz at 96 kHz. Set the coupling quick-charge register P0_R5 bits 5:4 to `11` for 50 ms *typical* duration and measure 10-uF settling before samples are trusted (TI SBAS892A pp.33,64,94). Confirm the complete register image/readback. The ADC's internal antialias filter is the audio-band antialias stage; the external 47-ohm/1-nF network is for RF/EMI.

Infineon v1.20 Table 6 (p.8) requires at least 25 kohm resistance on each mic output and at most 100 pF per output. The unity-buffer CMOS input avoids the ADC's 20-kohm *typical* direct load. TI OPA2320 SBOS513F pp.7-9 bounds input bias at 400 pA per channel at -40 to 125 C, while TI TPD2E2U06 SLLSEG9C specifies at most 10 nA leakage and 1.5 pF *typical* capacitance. Even taking the ESD leakage as a DC shunt at 1.35 V gives about 130 Mohm; the mic sees no intentional resistive load. A 75-pF harness/connector allocation plus the 4-pF typical op-amp common-mode input and 1.5-pF typical ESD capacitance sums to 80.5 pF before PCB parasitics. This **does not** prove 100 pF at the corner because the protection device publishes no maximum line capacitance, the mic's 250-ohm output impedance is typical, and the cable is not selected. The hardware port acceptance limit is 100 pF including all parasitics, measured on the actual harness.

With 20 kohm *typical* ADC input and 10 uF nominal coupling, the first-order high-pass corner is 0.796 Hz. At 5% low capacitance it is 0.838 Hz, giving 2.40 degrees of phase lead at 20 Hz. This is **not a worst-case low-frequency bound**: TI SBAS892A p.28 calls the ADC impedance typical and does not give a minimum. The 47-ohm/1-nF pole is 3.39 MHz nominal, above audio; the ADC internal antialias behavior still requires the chosen 96-kHz register profile. The mic's own typical 20-Hz acoustic cutoff and phase response (Infineon pp.6-7) are separate from the electrical high-pass calculation.

For an upper-level *linearity screen only*, Infineon's highest specified 94-dB SPL sensitivity of -37 dBV extrapolated to its **typical** 135-dB SPL overload point gives 1.585 Vrms differential. The ADC's 2-Vrms full scale at 3.3-V AVDD (TI SBAS892A pp.7,28) leaves 2.02 dB. A balanced leg would swing about 1.12 V peak about the mic's 1.35-V **typical** output DC, or roughly 0.23 to 2.47 V, inside a 3.25-V OPA supply even allowing its 45-mV worst output swing from rails at 2-kohm load (TI SBOS513F p.8). Infineon's AOP and output DC are typical, and sensitivity is specified at 94 dB SPL; this extrapolation cannot certify overload clipping or common-mode at all temperatures. Similarly, the 73-dB(A) mic SNR, 8.5-nV/âˆšHz OPA noise and 107-dB ADC SNR are at differing specified conditions, with ADC 48-kHz rather than 96-kHz data. Manufacturer-backed worst-case 96-kHz noise, response, clipping and phase bounds are required for R4-A closure of 001. The four-channel sweep over operating temperature and selected harness remains open as physical validation required by R14.

## 2. Native RT1062 boot: selected hardware baseline

Select `IS25WP064AJBLE`, 64 Mbit, 1.8 V, from the NXP MIMXRT1060-EVKB reference (user manual Table 17 p.20), on `NVCC_SD1=1.8 V`. Connect FlexSPI A CS/SCLK/IO0-3 to GPIO_SD_B1_06/07/08-11 as already allocated. NXP IMXRT1060RM Rev 4 pp.194,210-213 confirms this ROM pinout, 512-byte FCB at flash offset 0 and an initial 30-MHz read. Section 5 records the selected ISSI command and FCB field profile. LUT binary encoding and timing remain R4-A proof; the generated production image and hardware read/boot test remain firmware bring-up validation required by R14.

For unfused internal boot, set BOOT_MODE[1:0] to `10`: 4.7-kohm 1% pullup on MODE1 (GPIO_AD_B0_05) to NVCC_GPIO and 4.7-kohm 1% pulldown on MODE0 (GPIO_AD_B0_04). Reserve all BT_CFG0-11 pins and pull each down with 10 kohm for the zero FlexSPI NOR/3-byte initial read setting. The fixture enters ROM Serial Downloader mode `01` by pulling MODE1 to ground and MODE0 to the sensed powered NVCC_GPIO through 1-kohm 1% resistors before POR_B release. Section 5 proves static levels at adverse rail/resistor/internal-pull corners; transient capture and fixture drive still require schematic proof. Provide SWDIO E14, SWCLK F12, POR_B, ground and powered 3.3-V reference; fixture outputs must be high impedance whenever NVCC_GPIO is absent. UART1 TX/RX K14/L14 are the ROM recovery connection with 10-kohm pullups per NXP HW7 p.9. USB1 is outside the V1 recovery dependency. NXP RM pp.197-200 and HW7 pp.9-11 support the mode and pull strategy.

Use the `ECS-240-8-33B2-CKM-TR3` 24-MHz, 8-pF, 40-ohm maximum ESR crystal candidate between XTALI P11 and XTALO N11, with NXP's 2.2-Mohm XTALI-to-ground bias. A 12-pF capacitor at each crystal pin corresponds to 8-pF load only if pin/trace stray is 2 pF per side: `CL = 12/2 + 2 = 8 pF`. Release of the two capacitor values still requires a bounded stray-capacitance allocation and manufacturer-backed oscillator start, drive and USB-clock calculations. Physical clock startup and USB testing are due R14. Keep SWD as the default debug mode, JTAG_MOD and TRST_B at 4.7-kohm pulldown per HW7 pp.7-8.

R4-A2 freezes V1 at unfused internal boot: BT_FUSE_SEL and BOOT_CFG OTP fields remain factory-default, HAB remains open, and no production or field fuse burn is part of this circuit contract. Factory programming and field recovery use a powered service fixture that forces BOOT_MODE `01` before POR_B release, then communicates over ROM LPUART1; there is no V1 OTA or application update route and no dependence on USB recovery. SWD remains available for enclosed service/debug. This avoids a programmed-fuse recovery path that could disable the physical strap override. The complete FCB field layout, clock and other-rail reset circuits still need schematic proof. A tested boot image, measured POR waveform and dead-flash recovery run remain due R14. Finding 002 stays blocking for its remaining hardware requirements.

## 3. Board A portion of the power contract

Selected rail topology from a **required receiving envelope** of 4.5-5.5 V at Board A: `TPS62135RGXR` forced-PWM buck makes MCU_3V3 with 255-kohm top and 68.1-kohm bottom 1% feedback resistors; `TPS7A2033PDQNR` from received 5 V makes ADC/AFE_3V3; `TPS7A2028PDQNR` from MCU_3V3 makes MIC_2V8; `TPS7A2018PDQNR` from MCU_3V3 makes FLASH_SD1_1V8. The TPS7A20 parts use at least 1-uF *effective* input and output capacitance, EN tied to their parent rail unless a sequencing gate is proven necessary. The 2.8-V mic selection replaces the earlier nominal 2.75-V target and stays inside the Infineon 2.3-3.0-V operating range. TI TPS7A20 SBVS338H pp.5-7 gives the guaranteed output tolerance only at its VIN, load, capacitor and TJ conditions; this is why the calculation uses the buck's minimum voltage. All GPIO/EMC and HIGH/DCDC/ADC supply balls must be connected to their assigned powered domain; *_CAP outputs are decoupled and never used as external supplies (NXP CEC Rev 4 pp.26,30-31).

For the buck, TI TPS62135 SLVSBH3B pp.4-6 requires 3-17 V VIN, `VIN >= VOUT+1 V` for the +/-1% PWM feedback specification, 0.6-2.9 uH effective inductance, 6-200 uF effective output and at least 3 uF effective input capacitance. Select `XAL4020-102MEC`, 1 uH +/-20%, with [Coilcraft](https://www.coilcraft.com/en-us/products/power/high-voltage-inductors/xal/xal40xx/xal4020-102/) 14.6-mohm maximum DCR and 8.7-A 10%-drop Isat at 25 C. Specify 10-uF nominal X7R input (>=3-uF effective) and 2 x 22-uF nominal X7R local output (22-200-uF effective). Exact capacitor order codes/bias curves and hot inductor derating remain open. Adverse 1% divider, +/-1% FB voltage and 70-nA FB leakage yield **3.2185-3.4259 V**, within NXP's 3.0-3.6-V bank range. The maximum requires at least 4.426 V at the buck VIN for the documented PWM accuracy, so a 4.5-V Board A receiving minimum has only 74 mV margin before trace/ripple. This receiving requirement is **not yet guaranteed by Board B or the connector**. The LDO conditional ranges are MIC 2.758-2.842 V, flash 1.770-1.830 V and ADC/AFE 3.2505-3.3495 V. The TPS7A20 MIC 2.8-V accuracy needs VIN >=3.1 V, met by the calculated buck minimum; the 1.8-V part needs VIN >=2.3 V. ADC/AFE 3.3 V needs VIN >=3.6 V, met by the proposed 4.5-V receiving minimum. These DC bounds exclude ripple and transient excursions.

Tie VDD_SNVS_IN directly to VDD_HIGH_IN; there is no backup battery. For the RT1062 internal DCDC, select a 30-kohm 1% / 0.22-uF 10% DCDC_PSWITCH RC (5.881-7.333 ms component product) and `XFL4020-472MEC` 4.7-uH inductor. Coilcraft quotes 57.4-mohm maximum DCR and 2.0-A 10%-drop saturation current at 25 C; NXP HW7 pp.5-6 requires 4.7-10 uH, Isat >1 A and ESR <0.2 ohm. Specify **at least 33 uF effective** total on DCDC_OUT/VDD_SOC_IN in addition to every HW7 Table 2 pin-local capacitor group. Exact capacitor order codes/bias curves, hot inductor derating and the DCDC_IN ramp must be proven. NXP HW7 pp.4-5 also requires DCDC_IN reach 3.0 V within 0.3 RC, DCDC_PSWITCH reach 1.5 V at least 1 ms after DCDC_IN is stable, and POR_B remain low until all rails stabilize. An RC product alone cannot prove those relative edges.

Select `TPS389030DSER` to monitor MCU_3V3 and pull POR_B low with its open-drain RESET output. A 10-kohm pullup to the tied SNVS/HIGH_IN rail and a 10-nF 10% CT capacitor set the initial circuit. TI TPS3890 SLVSD65A pp.3-5 gives nominal falling/rising thresholds 2.890/2.907 V with +/-1% accuracy; adverse falling minimum is **2.861 V**, 261 mV above NXP's 2.6-V internal DCDC low-detect level, and adverse rising maximum is **2.936 V**, 282 mV below the calculated MCU_3V3 minimum. CT threshold/current limits imply roughly 7.8-15.8 ms release delay for the selected capacitance, before any other gate delay. The supervisor's MR input is reserved for an all-other-rails-valid gate; without that circuit and worst-case ramp/timing calculations, this is **only a verified MCU_3V3 threshold**, not a complete POR_B release proof. Measured waveforms remain due R14. The supervisor also needs powered-off RESET and pullup behavior checked against the RT1062 SNVS domain.

### 3.2 Regulator, dissipation and thermal screen

Set each TPS7A20 input and output to 4.7-uF nominal X7R with **>=1 uF effective** after voltage bias, tolerance and -40..85-C temperature. TI SBVS338H pp.5-7 specifies output accuracy for 1-300 mA, so add 2.7-kohm 1% to ground at ADC 3.3 V, 2.2-kohm 1% at MIC 2.8 V, and 1.5-kohm 1% at FLASH 1.8 V. At worst low output and high resistance these sink 1.192, 1.241 and 1.168 mA respectively. The three LDOs therefore meet the accuracy table's minimum-load condition even when devices sleep. Input/output cap order codes and effective-value curves still require selection. The RT1062 DCDC output target is 2 x 47-uF nominal X7R for >=33-uF effective total, plus each NXP HW7 Table 2 local capacitor; exact codes/derating remain open.

At 85-C **local** ambient and TI's JEDEC reference-board thermal resistances, ADC LDO dissipation is `(5.5-3.2505)*0.075 + 5.5*0.002 = 0.1797 W`, hence `TJ=114.85 C`, 10.15 C below 125 C. The 2-mA ground current is an imposed ceiling: TI's 2-mA figure is **typical** at 300-mA load, not a maximum. Mic and flash LDO screens are 0.0115/0.1062 W and 86.91/102.64 C respectively. With 350 mA and 3.4259 V, buck output is 1.1991 W. An **assumed**, acceptance-tested 70% minimum efficiency implies 0.5139-W converter loss, `TJ=104.73 C` on TI's 38.4-C/W RGX reference board and 20.27-C margin. TI does **not** guarantee a 70% floor or whole-converter loss at this corner. Reference thermal resistances are not guaranteed product-board values. Conservative package screens using NXP 12-mm RT1062 39.1-C/W, TI ADC RTW 32.6-C/W and OPA2320 DGK 174.8-C/W give 114.50, 91.01 and 87.58 C respectively; these assume the same reference-board heat path and the allocated package input powers. The ADC and OPA screens also depend on the imposed current ceilings.

TI TPS62135 SLVSBH3B pp.5-6 specifies 4.8-A minimum/6.5-A maximum high-side current limit and hiccup protection; its thermal shutdown is protection, not continuous operation. TI TPS7A20 SBVS338H p.7 gives 360-730-mA output-current limit under its stated test and a **typical-only** 160-mA hard-short current. At 5.5-V input, the ADC LDO's 730-mA fault-current upper value at 90% output could dissipate about `(5.5-2.97)*0.73 = 1.85 W` before shutdown, far above the continuous 0.1797-W screen; rail shorts must be handled by input-source current limiting/trace coordination and thermal shutdown without claiming a continuous safe junction. Size the buck inductor for the 6.5-A switch-limit corner: the selected XAL4020-102MEC has 8.7-A 10%-drop Isat at **25 C**, but its hot derating remains to be checked. Board A's 750-mA input ceiling and fault-energy limit must be enforced by the upstream Board B/interface design, outside this subtask.

**Exact remaining proof inputs for `012_a_load_power`:** (1) TI guaranteed four-channel 96-kHz, BCLK/FSYNC=128 ADC AVDD/IOVDD maxima, or formal acceptance of the 50/5-mA allocated ceilings with enforced load modes; (2) a TPS62135 guaranteed minimum efficiency/maximum loss at 4.5-5.5 V, 350 mA forced PWM, or an accepted 70% floor backed by product thermal tests; (3) exact capacitor MPN/bias/temperature curves proving all stated *effective* minima/maxima and hot Isat/DCR for both inductors; (4) product-board effective thermal resistances and local ambient demonstrating <=125-C junction for the regulator/MCU/ADC/OPA cases, plus maximum LDO ground-current bounds; (5) enforceable upstream current/fault-energy limit for the 750-mA receiving envelope. These prevent unconditional R4-A load/thermal closure today. R14 still requires measured 96-kHz/flash currents, transient, startup and enclosed-board thermal behavior; it cannot retroactively make a typical value a guaranteed maximum.

## 5. R4-A2 circuit work and remaining corner limits, 2026-10-01

**Boot straps and service policy.** NXP CEC Rev 4 Table 22 pp.37-38 guarantees GPIO `VIH >= 0.7 NVCC` and `VIL <= 0.3 NVCC`; an opposing on-chip 100-kohm pull may draw at most 48 uA, and input leakage adds at most 1 uA. Across the calculated MCU_3V3 range and 1% external 4.7-kohm straps / 1-kohm fixture overrides, the worst normalized normal high/low levels are 0.9277/0.0723, and forced recovery high/low levels are 0.8090/0.1871. Each clears its respective 0.7/0.3 threshold; opposing-strap current can reach 0.6071 mA. This proves **static** logic levels, not transient capture at POR_B. The powered fixture must be high impedance when NVCC_GPIO is absent. The unfused service policy in section 2 is now frozen; the `002_recovery_policy` requirement is closed while finding 002 remains open for other hardware.

**Flash load and ROM read.** NXP RM Rev 4 pp.212-213 says ROM fetches the 512-byte FCB at flash offset zero using its initial 30-MHz FlexSPI clock. The conservative first-stage profile is ISSI `03h` one-pad normal read with a 24-bit address, avoiding a quad-enable dependency. NXP RM Rev 4 Tables 9-15 and 9-18 pp.222-226 identify the FCB tag/version, one-pad serial-NOR type, internal sample clock, 30-MHz code and 8,388,608-byte A1 size; Table 9-16 assigns LUT indexes 0/1/3/5/9 to read/status/write-enable/erase/program. ISSI Rev A11 Table 8.1 gives opcodes `03h/05h/06h/20h/02h`; the selected field semantics are in `state/rt1062_pin_resource_map.json`. The exact LUT binary encoding, signal timing and firmware-produced 512-byte image are still unverified. ISSI section 9.3 p.94 rates normal read at 50 MHz to 12 mA maximum and program, status write and erase at 125 C to 30 mA maximum. The read-current footnote excludes output switching, so the **total** FLASH_SD1_1V8 load is still unbounded.

**AFE limit.** TI TLV320ADC5140 SBAS892A section 7.5 pp.7-10 gives only a *typical* 20-kohm input impedance and 48-kHz electrical SNR/current conditions; its section 8.3.6.7 specifies 96-kHz digital-filter response, which does not establish a full-temperature 96-kHz analog noise bound. No lower ADC input-impedance limit can be substituted into the 10-uF coupling pole. The existing 0.838-Hz and 2.40-degree figures therefore remain conditional. An ESD alternative, TI TPD4E1B06 Rev E section 5.5 p.5, states 0.95-pF maximum line capacitance, but only at 25 C and 2.5-V bias; it cannot establish the microphone's 100-pF maximum over the full operating corner. No ESD substitution is released. The 001 schematic electrical requirement remains open.

**Load, thermal and startup limits.** TI TLV320ADC5140 SBAS892A p.10 labels its 21.3-mA, four-channel 48-kHz AVDD consumption *typical* and publishes no guaranteed 96-kHz maximum. Section 3 now allocates 50 mA ADC AVDD and 5 mA ADC IOVDD as enforceable design ceilings, together with all other rail consumers, without claiming these are manufacturer maxima. At 75-mA ADC LDO output and the imposed 2-mA LDO ground-current ceiling, its reference-board screen is 114.85 C at 85-C ambient, conditional on assembled-board thermal resistance. For startup, NXP HW Guide Rev 7 pp.4-5 requires DCDC_IN reach 3.0 V within `0.3 RC`; with the selected component-tolerance minimum `RC=5.8806 ms`, this permits only **1.7642 ms**. TI TPS62135 SLVSBH3B section 9.4.6 and section 10.1.4.3 describe an approximately 200-us startup delay and typical 150-us fastest ramp; neither is a guaranteed loaded maximum. The selected RC alone cannot prove the rail ramp, the additional >=1-ms DCDC_PSWITCH delay, or all-rail POR_B release. Requirements `012_a_load_power` and `012_a_startup` remain open for the exact items in section 3 and the separate POR proof. No typical number or nominal reserve has been promoted to a guaranteed limit.
